# Code of conduct

The canonical, public version lives at
<https://malwarous.dev/code-of-conduct> (source:
[`public/code-of-conduct.html`](public/code-of-conduct.html)). This file is the
short repository version.

## The short version

1. **Permission first.** Only test systems you own or have explicit written
   permission to test. CTFs, our labs and authorized engagements are fair
   game. Everything else is not.
2. **Respect people.** No harassment, discrimination, doxxing or personal
   attacks. Beginners are welcome and questions are not a weakness.
3. **Protect privacy.** Do not share personal data, credentials, private
   messages or unpublished vulnerabilities.
4. **Disclose responsibly.** Report real vulnerabilities privately, give the
   owner time to fix them, and do not use them for profit or clout.
5. **Credit others.** Attribute write-ups, tools and ideas. No plagiarism and
   no flag-sharing during a live competition.
6. **Keep the room usable.** No spam, malware or unsolicited advertising.

This applies to the website, the arena, our events, pull requests, issues and
every space where you represent Malwarous.

## Reporting

Message an organizer in the [WhatsApp community](https://chat.whatsapp.com/KH2JPvkNBVl4ZqPslutRWs)
or contact the team directly. Reports are handled privately. Depending on
severity we may warn, mute, remove content or ban an account.

Maintainers are expected to follow this code too. A maintainer who cannot
handle a report fairly should step aside from that report.
