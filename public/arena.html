// 02 — how to play

~/arena/readme

walkthrough: from signing up to your first flag

Do steps 1 to 3 once. After that, every new room only needs steps 4 and 5.

  1. Create a free TryHackMe account

    Sign up at tryhackme.com/signup with Google or an email and password, and verify your email if asked. A free account is enough for every Malwarous room. Each player needs their own.

  2. Link your username to Malwarous

    Create your Malwarous account (or log in) and add your TryHackMe username, spelled exactly as it appears on TryHackMe. Rewards only go to linked members: it's how the room's scoreboard gets matched to you.

  3. Set your server to Asia Pacific (Mumbai)

    Do this before you start a machine: changing region shuts down running machines. On TryHackMe, click your account icon, then Manage Account, then VM and VPN Settings. Set Virtual Machine Region to Asia Pacific (Mumbai) and click Change Region. OpenVPN users: download your configuration file again from the same page.

  4. Join the room

    When a room goes live, its link appears in // 01 above and in our WhatsApp community. Open it and click Join Room. Read the tasks before you start a machine: free accounts get one hour of AttackBox time per day.

  5. Connect and solve

    Start the AttackBox or connect with OpenVPN (see below), then submit each answer on TryHackMe. Every correct answer adds to your score. When the room closes, the highest score ranks first; on a tie, whoever got there first.

Option A: AttackBox (easiest)

A Linux machine in your browser with the tools already installed. Nothing to install.

  1. Click Start AttackBox at the top of the room. It can take 2 to 3 minutes to load.
  2. Work in the split view, or click Open in a new tab for full screen.
  3. Click Shut down when you finish.
Option B: OpenVPN (your own computer)

Use this when your AttackBox hour runs out, or if you prefer your own Kali machine. Use the .ovpn file you downloaded after switching to Mumbai.

windows

  1. Install OpenVPN from openvpn.net/community-downloads.
  2. Open OpenVPN GUI as Administrator.
  3. Click the up arrow in the taskbar, then right-click the OpenVPN icon (a monitor with a plug).
  4. Choose Import, then Import file, and select your .ovpn file.
  5. Right-click the icon again and click Connect.

linux / kali

  1. Install OpenVPN: sudo apt install openvpn
  2. Connect: sudo openvpn ~/Downloads/<your-file>.ovpn
  3. Wait for Initialization Sequence Completed, and keep that terminal open while you play.
  4. Press Ctrl + C to disconnect.

macos

  1. Install OpenVPN for macOS and open it from the top bar.
  2. Right-click the icon, choose Import File, then Local file, and select your .ovpn file.
  3. Right-click the icon again and click Connect.

To check you're connected: on TryHackMe's VM and VPN Settings page, Connection shows a green Connected.

// 03 — rules

~/arena/rules

malwarous@qau:~$ cat rules.txt

  1. 01

    Play solo. One person, one TryHackMe account. No shared or team accounts.

  2. 02

    No spoilers. Don't share flags, answers or hints anywhere while a room is open, including our WhatsApp community, Discord and in person. Write-ups are allowed after the room closes.

  3. 03

    Attack only what the room gives you. Never TryHackMe's own systems, other players, or anything outside the room. We only hack with permission.

  4. 04

    No automated guessing. Don't script or spam answer submissions.

  5. 05

    Link your account. Only members whose TryHackMe username is on their Malwarous account can receive rewards.

  6. 06

    Play fair and stay kind.

Breaking a rule removes you from that month's rankings. Repeating it bans you from the arena. The organizers' decision is final, and TryHackMe's terms also apply.

// 04 — troubleshooting

tryhackme help ->
Common problems and fixes
problemfix
A target's website won't load in your own browserLab machines aren't on the public internet. Connect with OpenVPN or use the AttackBox.
Your AttackBox hour is used upSwitch to OpenVPN for the rest of the room.
OpenVPN is slow, or normal websites stop loadingCheck your home internet first. Then confirm your region is Mumbai and download a new configuration file.
OpenVPN fails on WindowsRun OpenVPN GUI as Administrator.
An old .ovpn file stops workingDownload a new configuration file from VM and VPN Settings.
Mumbai is unstable, or TryHackMe suggests another regionChange to the suggested region, then download a new configuration file if you use OpenVPN.
Shells don't connect back when you attack from a virtual machineRun OpenVPN inside that virtual machine, not on your main computer.
Your score isn't linked to youCheck that the TryHackMe username on your Malwarous account matches exactly.
