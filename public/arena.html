// 02 — how to play

~/arena/readme

walkthrough: from signing up to your first flag

  1. Create a free TryHackMe account

    Sign up at tryhackme.com. Then put your TryHackMe username on your Malwarous account: it's how we match the room's scoreboard to you for rewards.

  2. Know your AttackBox hour

    Free accounts get one hour of the AttackBox, TryHackMe's hacking machine in your browser. Read the task first, then start it when you're ready to work. Out of time? Connect your own Kali machine with TryHackMe's OpenVPN instead, which is free.

  3. Join the room

    When a room opens, its link appears in // 01 above (and on our Discord). Open it and click Join Room on TryHackMe.

  4. Solve the tasks

    Answer each question on TryHackMe. Every correct answer adds to your score on the room's scoreboard.

  5. Climb the board

    When the room closes, we take the final standings from its scoreboard: highest score first, and on a tie, whoever got there first.

// 03 — rules

~/arena/rules

malwarous@qau:~$ cat rules.txt

  1. 01

    Play solo. One person, one TryHackMe account. No shared or team accounts.

  2. 02

    No spoilers while a room is open. Don't share flags, answers or hints anywhere: Discord, WhatsApp, in person. Write-ups are welcome once the room closes.

  3. 03

    Only attack what the room gives you. Never TryHackMe's own systems, other players, or anything outside the room. We only hack with permission.

  4. 04

    No automated guessing. Don't script or spam answer submissions.

  5. 05

    Link your account to get rewarded. Rewards only go to members whose TryHackMe username is on their Malwarous account.

  6. 06

    Play fair, stay kind. Breaking a rule removes you from that month's rankings; doing it again bans you from the arena. The organizers' decision is final. TryHackMe's own terms apply too.
