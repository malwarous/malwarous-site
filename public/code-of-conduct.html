Code of conduct

We are a student community that takes systems apart to understand them. The same care applies to the people around us.

// 01 — the short version

last updated: 7 Nov 2026

malwarous@qau:~$ cat code-of-conduct.txt

  1. 01
    Permission first. Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. CTFs, our labs, and authorized engagements are fair game. Everything else is not. Curiosity is not consent.
  2. 02
    Respect people. No harassment, discrimination, doxxing, or personal attacks. Disagree with an idea as hard as you like; leave the person alone. Beginners are welcome, and questions are not a weakness.
  3. 03
    Protect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal data, credentials, private messages, or unpublished vulnerabilities. When in doubt, ask before you post.
  4. 04
    Disclose responsibly. Found a real vulnerability? Report it privately to the owner, give them time to fix it, and do not use it for profit or clout. We will help you write a clear report.
  5. 05
    Credit others. Share write-ups, tools, and ideas with attribution. Plagiarism and flag-sharing during a live competition undermine the point of the exercise.
  6. 06
    Keep the room usable. No spam, malware, or unsolicited advertising in community spaces. Keep chat on topic, and move long debugging threads somewhere sensible.

Something went wrong, or you saw someone cross a line? Message an organizer in the WhatsApp community or contact the team directly. Reports are handled privately. We may warn, mute, remove content, or ban an account depending on severity.

This applies to the website, the arena, our events, and every space where you represent Malwarous.

We only hack with permission.

CTFs, our own labs, and systems we're authorized to test. Nothing else.
