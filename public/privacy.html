Privacy policy

A short, specific account of what this site stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it.

This policy covers malwarous.dev only. It does not cover TryHackMe, WhatsApp, Google, GitHub or any other service you reach from here.

// 01

The short version

We collect what we need to run a student cybersecurity community: the details you give us when you make an account, the content you choose to post, and the technical records our hosting and database providers keep so the site stays online and secure.

We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising trackers. We do not use your email to send marketing. The public parts of the site are public on purpose: your handle, approved posts and guestbook messages can be seen by anyone.

If you want your account and its content removed, ask us. Section 08 explains how.

// 02

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, a password that Supabase stores in hashed form, your public handle, and the TryHackMe username you choose to add. We never see your raw password.
  • Sign-in data from Google or GitHub. If you choose an OAuth provider, we receive the account identifier and the basic profile information the provider releases, such as your email address. We do not receive your provider password.
  • Content you submit. Guestbook messages, write-ups, opportunity posts, event registrations and team applications. Approved public posts are displayed with your handle.
  • Technical records. Cloudflare, our host, processes request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested so it can deliver the site and block abuse. Supabase keeps authentication and database logs. These records are not used to build a marketing profile.
  • Live presence. If the site shows an online count, it uses an ephemeral Supabase Realtime channel. It reports a count, not a list of names, and disappears when you close the page.

We do not ask for your CNIC, student number, home address, phone number or payment details. Do not post those things in public spaces.

// 03

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, confirm your email, and let you sign in.
  • To publish the write-ups, opportunities and guestbook messages you choose to submit.
  • To manage event registration, team applications and the arena's community standings.
  • To moderate the community, investigate abuse and enforce the code of conduct.
  • To keep the site available, debug errors and protect it from attacks.

Our basis for this is your consent when you create an account or submit content, and our legitimate interest in running a safe community. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account, but that also means you can no longer sign in.

// 04

Local storage, not advertising cookies

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The site does use your browser's local storage for a few functional things:

  • Your theme choice. A preference is stored so the site does not flash the wrong colours on your next visit.
  • The boot animation flag. A session flag stops the short start-up sequence from repeating on every page load.
  • Your session. Supabase stores the authentication token that keeps you signed in. Clearing site data signs you out.

Cloudflare may set security cookies that are necessary to deliver the site and block malicious traffic. Your browser's settings let you remove local storage at any time.

// 05

Who else sees it

  • Supabase hosts the database and authentication service. Access rules in the database decide what an anonymous visitor, a signed-in member and an admin can read or write.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site and processes request logs as our hosting provider.
  • Google and GitHub are involved only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • TryHackMe and WhatsApp receive your data only when you follow a link to them. Their own policies apply from that point.
  • Admins. A small number of Malwarous admins can read team applications, review submitted posts and moderate the guestbook.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is necessary to protect someone's safety or the integrity of the site. We do not sell or rent personal data.

// 06

How long we keep it

Account details stay while your account exists. Public posts stay until you ask us to remove them or we decide to unpublish them. Team applications are kept while they are being reviewed and for up to 12 months afterwards, so we can answer questions about a decision. Security and server logs are kept for the short period our providers need to operate and protect the service.

When you ask us to delete an account, we remove the profile and the content tied to it. Some records may remain in backups, logs or moderation notes for a limited time, or longer where the law requires it.

// 07

How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS. Supabase hashes passwords and applies row-level security to the tables the site uses. Admin-only actions require an admin account, and the publishable Supabase key in the page is designed to be public: the database rules are what protect the data.

No system is perfect, and this one is run by students. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal data, we will tell affected people and take reasonable steps to contain it. Please report a suspected vulnerability privately rather than testing it in public.

// 08

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, export it, or delete it. You can also ask us to stop using your content in community posts, withdraw a submission, or close your account.

Send the request through the Malwarous WhatsApp community or the GitHub organization, and say clearly that it is a privacy request. We may ask you to confirm that you control the account before we act, so that someone else cannot delete your data.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can raise the issue with the relevant authority in Pakistan.

// 09

Students under 18

The site is built for university students and is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, use the site with the permission of a parent or guardian. Do not submit an application, guestbook message or public post containing personal information without that permission.

If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will remove it.

// 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes, when we add a service, or when the law changes. The effective date at the top of the page will move. If a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will announce it in the community where we can.

Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy. If you do not, stop using the site and ask us to delete your account.

// 11

Contact

The site is operated by the Malwarous team at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad. For a privacy question or request, reach an admin through the WhatsApp community or the GitHub organization.

See also the terms of service and the code of conduct.

We only hack with permission.

CTFs, our own labs, and systems we're authorized to test. Nothing else.
