// 03 — how we work

~/team/principles

malwarous@qau:~$ cat principles.txt

  1. 01

    Ship in public.

    The site, the challenges and the notes are built where people can see them. A rough working thing teaches more than a perfect plan in a private document.

  2. 02

    Permission first.

    Every lab, every challenge and every engagement has a boundary. We write it down, and we stay inside it. Curiosity is not consent.

  3. 03

    Teach what you learn.

    If you solve something interesting, turn it into a write-up, a workshop or a hint for the next person. The community gets stronger when knowledge does not stop at one laptop.

  4. 04

    Leave the door open.

    Beginners are not a nuisance. They are the reason the community exists. Answer the question, review the pull request, and make the next person's first step easier.
