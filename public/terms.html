Acceptable use

The short version: permission first, respect always. The full version is in the code of conduct, which forms part of these terms.

  • Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Our CTFs, arena rooms, labs and authorized engagements are in scope. Everything else is not.
  • Do not use the site to harass, threaten, dox, discriminate against or impersonate anyone.
  • Do not upload malware, phishing content, spam, or material that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Do not attempt to break the site's authentication, rate limits, access rules or hosting protections. If you find a vulnerability, report it privately and give us time to fix it.
  • Do not scrape personal data, share flags during a live competition, or publish someone else's private information.

We may remove content, restrict features or suspend an account when we reasonably believe these rules have been broken. Serious cases may be reported to the university or to law enforcement.
